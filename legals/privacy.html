Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2, 2026

At Smart Brain CBT, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our platform.

We Protect Your Data

Your information is encrypted and secured with industry-standard measures

No Data Selling

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties

You're in Control

Access, update, or delete your data anytime

1. Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide

When you create an account or use the platform, we may collect:

  • Full Name
  • Email address
  • Phone Number
  • Password (stored securely and encrypted)

b. Automatically Collected Information

We may automatically collect:

  • Device type and browser
  • IP address
  • Usage data (pages visited, time spent, actions taken)

This helps us improve performance and user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide exam practice and results
  • Track learning progress
  • Improve platform features and performance
  • Communicate important updates or support messages
  • Prevent fraud and abuse

We do not use your data for anything unrelated to Smart Brain CBT.

3. What We Do NOT Do (Very Important)

Our Privacy Promise

  • We do not sell your personal data
  • We do not rent your data to advertisers
  • We do not share your data for marketing without consent

Your data stays your data.

4. Data Sharing

We may share limited data only when necessary:

  • With trusted service providers (e.g., hosting, analytics, authentication)
  • To comply with legal obligations or lawful requests
  • To protect the rights, safety, or security of Smart Brain CBT and its users

All third parties are required to handle data responsibly.

5. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored securely using industry-standard protections
Passwords are encrypted
We take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or misuse

However, no system is 100% secure. You use the platform with this understanding.

6. Children's Privacy

Smart Brain CBT is intended for students aged 13 and above.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If such data is discovered, it will be deleted promptly.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

Access

View your personal data

Update

Correct your information

Delete

Request account deletion

Export

Request your data

Requests can be made through our contact details below.

8. Cookies and Tracking

We may use cookies or similar technologies to:

Keep users logged in

Improve platform performance

Understand usage patterns

You can control cookies through your browser settings.

9. Data Retention

We retain user data only for as long as:

  • Your account is active
  • It is needed to provide services
  • Required by law or legitimate business needs

Inactive accounts may be deleted after a reasonable period.

10. Third-Party Links

Smart Brain CBT may contain links to external websites.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Continued use of Smart Brain CBT means you accept the updated version.
