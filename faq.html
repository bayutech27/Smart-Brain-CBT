General Questions

Basic information about Smart Brain CBT

1. What is Smart Brain CBT?

Smart Brain CBT is an online computer-based test platform designed to help students prepare for exams like WAEC, NECO, and JAMB.

It allows students to:

  • Practice thousands of exam-style questions
  • Take timed mock examinations
  • Get familiar with real CBT exam formats
  • Track progress with detailed analytics
  • Identify and improve weak subject areas

The platform simulates real exam conditions to help build confidence and improve performance before the actual exam day.

2. Is Smart Brain CBT an official WAEC, NECO, or JAMB platform?

Important Notice:

No. Smart Brain CBT is not affiliated with:

WAEC
NECO
JAMB

We are an independent learning and practice platform created to help students prepare better for their examinations.

Our questions are designed to match exam patterns and syllabus, but we are not an official examination body.

3. Can Smart Brain CBT guarantee my exam success?

Honest Answer: No.

No platform can honestly guarantee exam success. Smart Brain CBT helps you:

Practice extensively
Build speed and accuracy
Gain confidence
Manage time effectively

However, your final result depends on multiple factors:

  • Your preparation and study habits
  • Consistent practice and effort
  • Understanding of concepts
  • Exam conditions and environment
  • Physical and mental readiness on exam day

We provide the tools for success, but the effort must come from you.
